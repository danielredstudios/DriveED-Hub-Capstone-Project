# Driving School Management System - Evaluation Questionnaire

This questionnaire is designed to evaluate the system against its core objectives using a 4-point Likert Scale.

---

## Rating Scale

- **1: Strongly Disagree** (Feature is missing or fails to meet requirements)
- **2: Disagree** (Feature is present but difficult to use or inconsistent)
- **3: Agree** (Feature works as intended and meets the operational goal)
- **4: Strongly Agree** (Feature is exceptionally well-implemented and exceeds needs)
